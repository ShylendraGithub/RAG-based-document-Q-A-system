# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts. Passwords are rotated only after a suspected compromise, not on a fixed schedule.

## Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may not store customer data. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 2 hours.

## Incident reporting

Report suspected security incidents to security@lumen.example or in the #security-incident Slack channel. Do not attempt to investigate on your own. The security team acknowledges reports within 30 minutes during business hours.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data is Restricted and may only be accessed through approved tools. Access to Restricted data is logged and reviewed quarterly.
