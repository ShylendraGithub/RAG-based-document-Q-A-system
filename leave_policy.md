# Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Lumen Analytics, a fictional company used for this demo.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year, and carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over to the next year.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Requesting leave

Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance for absences of 5 days or more. Managers must respond to a leave request within 3 working days.
