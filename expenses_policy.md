# Travel and Expense Policy

## Eligible expenses

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business travel, including flights, accommodation, ground transport and meals. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours, and premium economy is permitted for longer flights.

## Meal allowance

The daily meal allowance is $60 when travelling domestically and $85 when travelling internationally. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Submitting claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense using the Finance app. Attach itemised receipts for any expense over $25. Claims submitted more than 90 days after the expense will be rejected. Approved claims are paid in the next monthly payroll run.

## Approvals and hotels

Any single expense over $500 needs prior written approval from a department head. Hotel bookings must use the preferred booking platform. The nightly hotel cap is $220 in most cities and $320 in New York, London and Tokyo.
