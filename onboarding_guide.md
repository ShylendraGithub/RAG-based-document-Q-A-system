# New Hire Onboarding Guide

## First week

On day one, new hires collect their laptop from IT at 9:30 and meet their buddy, an experienced colleague who answers day-to-day questions for the first 90 days. Orientation sessions run from Monday to Wednesday.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months. A formal review takes place at the end of month 3 and again at the end of month 6.

## Working hours and location

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager agreement. The main office is in Pune, with satellite hubs in Berlin and Austin.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of $1,200 per year for courses, books and conferences. Learning budget claims are submitted through the same Finance app as expenses.
